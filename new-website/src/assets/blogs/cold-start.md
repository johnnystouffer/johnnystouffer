New codebase, new people, new acronyms. Here's what actually helped.

- Ask the *dumb* question in week one. It gets expensive later.
- Keep a `questions.md`. Half of them answer themselves.
- Ship something tiny in the first two weeks.

> The goal isn't to look smart. It's to become useful fast.

---

**Days 1–30:** read. **Days 31–60:** fix. **Days 61–90:** own.
