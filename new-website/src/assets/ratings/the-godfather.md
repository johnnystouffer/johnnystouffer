Nothing in this movie is in a hurry, and that's the point. The wedding alone takes its time introducing every player, so when things fall apart you already know exactly who everyone is and what they stand to lose.

## What holds up

- Brando gets the iconography, but it's Pacino's slow turn that carries the film.
- Gordon Willis's shadows: half the faces are in the dark, and it says more than the dialogue.
- The baptism sequence is still one of the best pieces of editing ever put on screen.
