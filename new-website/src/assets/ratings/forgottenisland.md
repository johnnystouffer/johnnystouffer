When I first saw a trailer for this movie I immediately wrote it off, that was until the end of the trailer wher I read ***From the creators of The Last Wish***. That line alone made me buy tickets *(If you have never seen [The Last Wish](https://letterboxd.com/film/puss-in-boots-the-last-wish/) you NEED to)*. Despite the weird trailer and [awful box office performance](https://www.slashfilm.com/2271162/forgotten-island-acclaimed-2026-animated-movie-box-office-flop/) I went and watched it. 

I do not regret watching it at all though, it was a great movie. Some of the best animation I have ever seen, additionally there was great emotional depth, the climax to the story was executed amazingly, and most importantly, I had a great time watching it.

A couple of things the movie did wrong, for one the **marketing**, the trailers and promotions did not do it justice at all, I thought it would be bad looking at the promotional material, and secondly what bothered me the most, **the pacing was too high energy, low attention span, tiktok brained.** You cannot even process the joke before the next joke already has happened, the middle of the movie was so fast its hard to recall what happened, even right when the movie just ended. This pacing dies down around halfway through thankfully.

Overall a great movie that deserved to do way better than its box office performance. If you haven't seen it you should watch it.
