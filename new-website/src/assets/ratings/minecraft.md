In second grade while watching a school play, I was being a typical kid and not paying attention to the play when I saw my friends older brother playing a game on his iPod. I had never seen it before but it intrigued me, I immediately asked what he was playing and he said he just got it and it was called **Minecraft**

That night I went home and immediately went to YouTube to see people playing this game, that is where I found a youtuber called *CaptainSparklez*. From that moment I watched Minecraft daily, until I finally convinced my dad to get it for me on my Xbox 360.

Eventually I decided I needed to be like the youtubers I watched and get *Java Edition*. Trying to run it on our janky old HP laptop then later our family Toshiba laptop. I spent so many hours not just playing this game in survival, but on the servers, on the mods, on trying everything to get my computer to run fast so I could use shaders, so much so I realized how much I loved not just gaming on computers, but that I loved working on computers and learning how they work. 

I don't think there was a single month of since I got the game that I did not play it. So much of my childhood has something to do with Minecraft, I still play this game to this day with my friends. They have times where they tend to not play as much taking month long breaks, but I never really do, no matter how much I play this game I always find something that brings me back. 

I absolutely loved and still to this day love this game and all the memories it has brought. **There will never be a game that could ever do what Minecraft has done.**

If you have never seen it, please watch [JSchlatt's Minecraft Tribute](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zNZ1rq5kW4M). It shows really why this game captured our generation.
