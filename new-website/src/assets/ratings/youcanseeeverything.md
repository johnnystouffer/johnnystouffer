I really wanted to watch this movie, I waited a long time to see it just so I could watch it with my friends back at Michigan State, and it did not dissapoint.

This was the kind of movie you sit in your car with your friends and argue about it for hours, cause this movie really gave nothing concrete and everything for your interpretation.

*Do you think Elizabeth is too dumb to communicate correctly or is she a master manipulator?*

*Is Billy an evil oppurtunistic capitalizing off her exploitation or is does he completely believe her?*

*Did Nathan Fielder slowly take her side and make it an anti Billy movie off of bias?*

*Did Nathan by end really actually start to like her?*

At the end of the day I did not view this much as a documentary and more as a mind trip. [The whole plan to make this a documentary with her reaching out to A24 to show how human she was is already a red flag itself](https://www.vanityfair.com/story/you-can-see-everything-nyff-nathan-fielder-elizabeth-holmes). The second we could no longer actually record Nathan's interactions with Elizabeth was the second the credibility dropped off.

This was a great movie though, I was locked in on the movie the whole time and it did not even feel anywhere close to 3 hours, every scene just made you question what you thought from last scene only to then jump to something else that makes you rethink it all again.

My main critiques of this movie are I did not like that Amanda Seyfried was in the movie, I get its because she played her in the show but it just made the movie worse in my opinion even though her conversation with Billy was probably the funniest part of the movie. I also felt that the second half was just not as informative and more of a hit piece of Billy than anything else (not that I like Billy but her is only a piece of the problem)

Overall, I was entertained and I highly suggest people watch this movie, but ***DO NOT*** expect an actual reliable documentary as it is not credible at all.
