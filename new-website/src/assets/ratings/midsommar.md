I am not a horror fan by any means, there are very few horror movies I genuinely like. So when I was told to watch this movie I did not think I would like it at all. But this one surprised me, it was an unorthodox horror movie, more so a psych drama than a horror movie.

I really like the unorthodox techniques the movie used, for example the color. Horror movies are notoriously dark to set the tone whereas this movie opted to be incredibly bright and appear happy. Additionally I liked the dynamic between the characters. The characters themselves were pretty unlikable besides Dani, but how they interacted and how their relationships evolved throughout was well written, especially the interactions between Dani and Christian. Speaking on Dani I thought Florence Pugh's performance was absolutely amazing.

However there was quite a bit I did not like. For one the pacing, this was a 2.5 hour movie that couldve been done in 1.5 hours. Additionally the climax didn't feel much like a climax, she comes back finds him cheating and then kills him and boom movie done. Also, again I just am not a fan of the genre and these types of movies as a whole. I just don't find them entertaining or emotionally moving.

Overall I don't really know if I like this movie. It was good for a horror movie and I appreciate the unorthodox style it used, but the pacing and climax being very underwhelming brought it down. I found this movie very interesting and **I can appreciate it which is why I give it a decent rating, but I can't say I like it, nor can I say I dislike it.**
