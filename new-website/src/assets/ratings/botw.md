I stopped playing Nintendo games after I was about 13 or 14 years old. Do not get me wrong I loved the *Wii* and my *DSi*, but as I got older, Nintendo seemed like the younger kid brand, why play Nintendo when I got an *Xbox 360, an iPod touch, and PC games.* I left Nintendo games thinking that era of my life was gone.

Until high school, when my parents got me a Nintendo Switch with two games, *Super Mario Odyssey, and Breath of the Wild*. Still thinking I grew out of Nintendo, I thought I was rarely gonna play my switch besides *Super Smash Bros*. Then sometime during Covid I decided to try Breath of the Wild, so I hopped off call with my friends to try it out. My friends did not hear from me for the next month. 

I have never had a game grab me like the way Breath of the Wild did, the open world and the amount of things to see and do, **it felt endless, it felt overwhelming, but in the best way possible**. I would get so excited getting to new towers to unlock a new section of the map, I loved solving the mini puzzles on each shrine, I loved every little side quest that would distract me for hours.

The ONLY complaint I have about this game is I am tired of the Korok seeds, and I wish the boss fights and dungeons were harder or that there were more of them. 

Otherwise, this game absolutely blew me away and there is not a thing I would not do to play this game for the first time again.
