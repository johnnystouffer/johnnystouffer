I was introduced to this series via its theme song [A Cruel Angels Thesis](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nU21rCWkuJw&list=RDnU21rCWkuJw&start_radio=1), which I believe, to this day, is one of the **BEST** anime openings of all time. And knowing that it's a well-loved psychological drama anime, I thought there was no way I could dislike this show.

**But god no was I wrong.**

The anime starts its first few episodes learning about the characters, with a repeated theme of fighting angels. It was pretty boring in my opinion, but thats fine; a lot of anime are slow burners and I did not watch the show for the mech suits or fight scenes, so I continued, thinking the best was yet to come.

However, as you get further along, one of the main reasons I hate this show really starts to shine... **Shinji**.

The character writing for Asuka, Rei, and Misato, were all okay in my opinion, but Shinji completely ruined any chance for me to enjoy this show. I understand that he is portrayed as struggling with severe depression, but a show should handle a deep topic like in a way that is consumable and emotionally moving, which it did not do. Following his thoughts and actions each episode only made me want to stop watching.

Additionally, the last few episodes felt like they were just **cryptic to be cryptic**. It felt like a desperate attempt to be intellectual and artistic. The film *End of Evangelion* wasn't any better either; it was frustrating and, again, not moving or entertaining to watch.

To rub salt in the wound, a lot of fans dismiss criticism of the show. From what I've experienced, many fans argue against every reason someone dislikes it, unable to accept that the show may have executed something poorly or that an individual has an opinion. No, instead, it is because *"you just don't understand"* or *"you just cannot appreciate a complex show"*. Not all fans of Evangelion are like this, of course, but enough are to make the show seem ***incredibly pretentious***, which was another huge turnoff.

I understand why people enjoy this anime. I get why people will defend it. But I personally really disliked this show. I look down on it mostly because the potential it had was *insane*; with its concepts, pscyhology, and theme, everything could've gone right. **Yet none of it delivered.**

**I have never been more disappointed in a show.**
