Words cannot describe how dissapointed I was with this anime. I was introduced to this series via its theme song [A Cruel Angels Thesis](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nU21rCWkuJw&list=RDnU21rCWkuJw&start_radio=1) which I still believe to this day is one of the **BEST** anime openings of all time.

And with got we got a psych drama anime that is well loved, there was no way I could've disliked this show.

**But god no was I wrong.**

We open the first few episodes with a repeated theme of fighting angels and learning about the characters. Pretty boring in my opinion but thats fine, a lot of anime are slow burners and I did not watch the show for the mech suits or fights, so I thought the best was yet to come.

But then we get further into the show and the one of the main reasons I hate this show really starts to shine... **Shinji**.

I thought Asuka, Rei, Misato, were all okay written characters I guess. But Shinji ruins any chance of me liking this show. I get that it is supposed to represent severe depression, but a show needs to turn a deep topic like that into consumable media that is moving, which this did not do, it just made me want to stop watching.

Plus the last episodes just felt **cryptic to be cryptic**. Whether the studio ran out of money or not it felt like a desperate attempt to be intellectual and artistic. The *End of Evangelion* was not amy better either, just frustrating and not again, not moving or entertaining to watch.

To rub salt in the wound a lot of fans dismiss criticism of the show, every reason you dislike the show is not because the show didn't do something well or because, I don't know, it's our opinion? No, instead it is because *"you just don't understand" or "you just cannot appreciate a complex show"*. First of all not all NGE fans are like this of course, but enough are to make the show seem ***incredibly pretentious*** which was another huge turnoff.

I get why people like the show, I get why people will defend it, but I just really disliked this show. I look down on it more because the potential it had was *insane*, the concepts, the pscyhology, the theme, everything could've gone right. **Yet none of it delivered. I have never been more dissapointed in a show**
